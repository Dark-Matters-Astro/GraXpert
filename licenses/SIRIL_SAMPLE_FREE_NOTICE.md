# Siril sample-free background method

GraXpert's sample-free background model is adapted from the automatic-gradient
implementation in Siril. The GraXpert team thanks Cyril Richard and the Siril
team for their work and for supporting continued collaboration between both
projects.

- Siril documentation: https://siril.readthedocs.io/en/latest/processing/background.html#automatic-sample-free-method
- Siril source: https://gitlab.com/free-astro/siril/-/blob/master/src/algos/background_extraction.c
- Revised simplified model: https://gitlab.com/free-astro/siril/-/commit/5555df299f96923551bcc093c0e5e1a022648061
